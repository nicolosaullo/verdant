**Horticulture**

Brassicas hold water demand surprisingly low in cool, dim autumn weather because transpiration is driven by both temperature and radiation, not heat alone. Wind complicates this by stripping humid air from around the leaves, which can increase water loss even when temperatures are not high. In maritime autumns, wind often matters more than headline temperature for brassica water use.
